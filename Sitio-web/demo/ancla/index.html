Questions you might have

Can’t find your answer? Write to us — we reply within 24 hours.

How long does a session last?

Each session lasts 50 minutes. Most people start with one session per week and space them out as they feel better.

Is online therapy as effective as in-person?

Research shows online therapy can be just as effective for most concerns. Choose the format where you feel most comfortable.

What happens in the first session?

We talk about what brings you here, what you’d like to change and how we can work together. No pressure to share more than you want.

Is everything confidential?

Yes. Everything you share is protected by professional confidentiality and data protection law.

How many sessions will I need?

It depends on each person and goal. We review progress together regularly so you always know where you stand.
